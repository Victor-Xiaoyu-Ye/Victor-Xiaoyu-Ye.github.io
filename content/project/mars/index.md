Developed a simulator for complex dynamic outdoor driving scenes using compositional neural radiance fields, and led implementation of the [MARS code framework](https://github.com/open-air-sun/mars) on [NeRFStudio](https://nerf.studio).

The associated paper, *MARS: An Instance-aware, Modular and Realistic Simulator for Autonomous Driving*, was presented at CICAI 2023 and received the **Best Paper Runner-up Award**.
