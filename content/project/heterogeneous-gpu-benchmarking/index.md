Developing a reproducible benchmarking framework for heterogeneous GPU clusters, spanning single-GPU, intra-node multi-GPU, inter-node multi-GPU, and cluster-wide parallelism. The framework assesses computation, communication, and system coordination to establish performance baselines and guide optimization, with validation on representative workloads such as Qwen 7B.
